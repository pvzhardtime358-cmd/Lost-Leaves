Mod Information

Lost Leaves is a comprehensive PvZ 2 fan mod that restores the original vanilla features removed in newer versions of the game. Our goal is to preserve classic gameplay while expanding it for longtime fans who loved the original elements.

  • All restored vanilla PvZ 2 features from older versions
  • Unique mechanics and challenges not present in the official game
  • Chinese plants and zombies integrated into standard gameplay
  • Custom-designed plants and zombies with unique abilities
  • Enhanced gameplay balance for both casual and advanced players

Lost Leaves is made for true PvZ fans who want to experience a nostalgic, yet deeply expanded, version of the game. It combines classic elements with fresh content, offering hundreds of hours of fun, strategy, and creative combinations.
